## 1. Project Goal
Develop a receive-only antenna system that uses existing Wi-Fi or Bluetooth signals for environmental sensing and improved directional reception. The design must be practical, reconfigurable, measurable, and within the contest budget of US$1,500.

---

## 2. Work Plan and Timeline

| Period | Main Activities | Expected Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1:** 21–26 October | Learn antenna basics, RF communication, propagation, ISAC, and contest rules. | Each member presents key findings. |
| **Week 2:** 27 October – 2 November | Research possible designs, antenna arrays, beam switching, polarization, and sensing methods. | Compare possible approaches. |
| **Week 3:** 3–9 November | Review the ideas with the mentor. Compare ideas, pick one main design and any optional upgrade. Check equipment, software, costs, and technical feasibility. | Select and document the project design. |

| **Week 7:** 1–7 December | Draft the four-page proposal, including the design, budget, and evaluation plan. | First proposal draft. |
| **Week 8:** 8–14 December | Obtain mentor feedback and revise the proposal. | Reviewed proposal. |
| **Week 9:** 15–21 December | Final checks and submission. | Proposal submitted before the deadline. |

## 3. Team Responsibilities
* **Antenna and Simulation (Rooney & Charles):** Study antenna designs, model the proposed system, and investigate beam patterns.
* **Software and Sensing (Michael):** Explore signal processing, movement detection, data collection, and visualization.
* **Hardware and Integration (Winnie & Charles):** Investigate components, receiver options, switching methods, and costs.
* **All Members:** Study the fundamentals, share findings, review design decisions, and contribute to documentation.
* **Faculty Mentor (Dr. Smaida):** Guide technical decisions and review progress.

---

## 4. Weekly Meeting Routine
Meet every Monday to:
* Present what each member has learned.
* Review progress and unresolved problems.
* Agree on tasks for the following week.
* Record decisions, resources, and results on GitHub.

---

## 5. Key Feasibility Checks
Before committing to the complete design, the team should verify that:
- [ ] The receiver can reliably collect useful Wi-Fi or Bluetooth measurements.
- [ ] Movement can be detected in repeatable tests.
- [ ] The proposed antenna configuration can change its reception pattern as intended.
- [ ] Both sensing and communication objectives can be demonstrated with measurable results.
- [ ] The complete system fits within the US$1,500 budget.

---

## 6. After Proposal Submission
If selected to continue, the team will build the prototype, measure its performance, integrate the sensing and communication demonstrations, and prepare the final report and demonstration materials by May 2027.
