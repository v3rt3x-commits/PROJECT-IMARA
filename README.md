# IMARA-ISAC
Development of a reconfigurable Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC) antenna system for wireless sensing, directional communication, and interference suppression — 2027 IEEE AP-S Student Design Contest
### Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC) Antenna System

**Competition:** 2027 IEEE AP-S Student Design Contest  
**Team:** 3 Students + 1 Faculty Mentor  
**Project Status:** Research, Design and Development

---

## 1. Overview

IMARA-ISAC is a student engineering project focused on exploring **Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC)**, an emerging wireless technology that combines communication and environmental sensing within a shared system.

Traditional wireless systems primarily transmit and receive information. ISAC extends this capability by investigating how wireless signals can also provide information about objects, movement, and changes in the surrounding environment.

Our goal is to investigate antenna designs, signal-processing techniques, and system architectures that could support both reliable wireless communication and sensing.

## 2. Problem Statement

Modern wireless applications increasingly require efficient communication, environmental awareness, and effective use of limited spectrum resources.

Conventional communication and sensing systems often use separate hardware or operate independently. ISAC offers a potential approach to integrating these functions.

This project will investigate the technical challenges involved in developing an antenna system capable of supporting both functions while considering interference, signal quality, design complexity, and practical implementation.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Study the fundamental principles of antennas, electromagnetic waves, and wireless signal propagation.
- Investigate antenna configurations suitable for integrated sensing and communications.
- Explore how received wireless signals can be processed to identify changes in the environment.
- Evaluate directional communication and interference-reduction techniques where appropriate.
- Develop simulations, design models, and experimental demonstrations as the project progresses.
- Document design decisions, test procedures, results, limitations, and lessons learned.

## 4. Proposed Technical Approach

The team will investigate the following areas:

### A. Antenna Design
Study antenna parameters such as resonant frequency, gain, radiation patterns, bandwidth, polarization, and impedance matching.

### B. Signal Transmission and Reception
Explore how electromagnetic signals propagate, interact with objects, and are received by an antenna system.

### C. Signal Processing
Investigate suitable techniques for analyzing received signals and identifying meaningful changes associated with the environment.

### D. Sensing and Communication Integration
Examine possible architectures for sharing antenna resources or signal-processing components between sensing and communication functions.

### E. Simulation and Testing
Use appropriate simulation software and, where feasible, practical measurements to evaluate performance against defined requirements.

*The final architecture and implementation methods will be selected following research, feasibility analysis, and design evaluation.*
